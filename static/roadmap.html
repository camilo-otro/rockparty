Rock the House

Product Roadmap

An app for organizing jam sessions — toques — among musicians: line up a venue, build the setlist together, and see who's playing, running the show, or coming to watch. Spanish UI, mobile-first, dark theme.

Updated 4 Sep 2026  ·  Phases 0–1 shipped  ·  Phase 2 nearly done  ·  Phase 4's Bands epic jumped the queue and shipped  ·  everything built is live  ·  SvelteKit + Supabase, on Netlify

✓ Bands — shipped & live · the whole epic, #40 + 7 issues
  • ✓#40 · 70
    Bands as a first-class thingKeystone
    A band is now its own entity with its own roster and permissions — not just a habit some musicians have. Everything below hangs on it.
  • ✓#71 · 72
    Create, manage, and show off a bandDesign
    Start a band, set the lineup and who plays what, name managers. Each band gets a public page with its members and its gig history.
  • ✓#78
    Bandmates who aren't on the app yet
    A manager can list a member by name and instrument without them having an account — the lineup is complete from day one instead of half-empty while people sign up.
  • ✓#75
    Band avatarsDesign
    Upload a photo, crop it in-app; it's resized and compressed in the browser before it ever leaves the phone, which keeps hosting inside the free tier.
  • ✓#73 · 74
    Sign up as a band, approved as a unitDesign
    A band claims a song in one move — no filling five slots one by one — and the organizer says yes or no to the whole band at once. The setlist then shows the band, not five separate names.
  • ✓—
    Band sets, collapsedDesign
    A band's consecutive songs group into one set block with the lineup shown once, collapsible to a summary line — “3 canciones · ~18 min”, estimated from song lengths plus a minute between each. Open jam songs stay one continuous list.
  • ✓#76
    Test bands stay out of real toques
    The #67 test-data rules extended to bands, including a guard so a test band can't sign up for a real event.
✓ Songs & setlist — shipped & live · pulled forward from #45
  • ✓#80 · 83
    Add songs from SpotifyHas spec
    The old flaky autocomplete is gone. Search Spotify from inside the app and tap a result — title, artist, artwork, length and the link all come with it. Remaster tags are stripped and remix/live versions filtered out; a manual entry stays for anything not on Spotify.
  • ✓#82
    Search that finds the song you meant
    “one metallica” now returns Metallica's One first. Title and artist are matched together, exact titles rank top, and short names like U2 work.
  • ✓#77

For the design team

The visual language is established and dark-only. Figma and the coded tokens together are the source of truth — the app has intentionally evolved past some Figma frames, so mine them for the language, not per-screen fidelity.

Foundations

Type — Roboto Condensed, light weights by default; headings UPPERCASE with a touch of tracking.

Brand — the purple→orange gradient wordmark, used sparingly for hero moments.

Frame — mobile-first (Figma at 393px), dark theme only, Spanish copy in the app's voice.

Vocabulary — list rows, section headings, pills/badges, the primary purple CTA, status badges, and instrument-slot icons.
