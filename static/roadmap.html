Rock the House

Product Roadmap

An app for organizing jam sessions — toques — among musicians: line up a venue, build the setlist together, and see who's playing, running the show, or coming to watch. Spanish UI, mobile-first, dark theme.

Updated 25 Aug 2026  ·  Phases 0–1 shipped  ·  Phase 2 in progress, everything built is live  ·  SvelteKit + Supabase, live on Netlify

For the design team

The visual language is established and dark-only. Figma and the coded tokens together are the source of truth — the app has intentionally evolved past some Figma frames, so mine them for the language, not per-screen fidelity.

Foundations

Type — Roboto Condensed, light weights by default; headings UPPERCASE with a touch of tracking.

Brand — the purple→orange gradient wordmark, used sparingly for hero moments.

Frame — mobile-first (Figma at 393px), dark theme only, Spanish copy in the app's voice.

Vocabulary — list rows, section headings, pills/badges, the primary purple CTA, status badges, and instrument-slot icons.
